## Why

The checklists destination (`/es/checklists`, `/en/checklists`, `/pt/checklists`) is one of the two remaining `registerPlaceholderScreen` modules: it renders the shared "not yet available" partial and nothing else. Every capability around it is now real — `aircraft-fleet` owns the pilot's aircraft, `flight-intent` owns the planned flight, `preflight-risk-assessment` scores it, and `home-dashboard` assembles the brief — and the home screen's Checklists tile is the last tile that can only say the feature does not exist. A pre-flight decision-support tool whose pre-flight checklist is a placeholder is incomplete in the most literal way.

The prototype (`diseno/src/App.tsx`, `Checklists`, lines 383–524) defines the visual target: a three-pane layout of fleet → checklist selector (normal procedures and a red-banded emergency group) → item list with checkboxes, a `n / m` completion counter, a progress bar, a reset control, and an all-complete confirmation. Its content is a hardcoded sample shared by all three sample aircraft. This change ports the **layout and interaction model** and replaces the hardcoded content with per-aircraft, pilot-owned data.

## What Changes

- Replace `checklistsModule`'s `registerPlaceholderScreen` with a real checklists screen registered under the localized destination for each supported locale.
- **Checklist library, per aircraft, owned by the pilot.** Creating an aircraft seeds it with a built-in **generic GA template** — six normal procedures (Preflight/Walkaround, Before Start, Before Takeoff, Cruise, Before Landing, Shutdown/Securing) and three emergency procedures (Engine Failure — Airborne, Engine Fire — In-Flight, Electrical Fire) — copied into pilot-owned rows. From that point the rows are the pilot's: the template is never re-applied and never edits them.
  - The seeded content is **explicitly labeled generic and not the aircraft's POH/AFM**, in text, on every screen that shows it, until the pilot has edited it. It is sample-grade content under the standing provenance rule, not an authority.
  - Full CRUD: create, rename, reorder, and delete checklists; add, edit, reorder, and delete items within a checklist. Aircraft created before this change are backfilled with the same seed by the migration.
- **Normal checklists are runnable; emergency checklists are reference-only.** Selecting a normal checklist for the pilot's next flight intent opens a **run**: each item toggle is an htmx `POST` that persists, so progress survives reload, sign-out, and a change of device. Emergency checklists render as read-only procedure text with the prototype's red band and its "confirm each action positively" notice, and have no checkboxes, no run, and no progress state — an emergency is not a record-keeping event, and a half-ticked engine-fire list is worse than none.
- **A run snapshots the items it was started from.** Each run copies every item's text and ordinal at start time (the precedent `risk_assessments.aircraft_snapshot` sets). A later edit to the checklist never rewrites an in-progress or completed run, so what a completed run shows is what the pilot actually read and confirmed.
- **Runs are scoped to a flight intent.** A run belongs to `(pilot, flight_intent, checklist)`; at most one open run per pair. Confirming the last item marks the run complete and records the completion time. Reset clears the item states of the *open* run; it never deletes a completed one.
- **History.** Completed runs are kept and listed on a per-aircraft history sub-route (mirroring `risk`'s `/historial`), showing the checklist name, the flight intent it belonged to, the completion time, and the snapshotted items as confirmed.
- **Home tile becomes real.** The Checklists tile reports the run of the aircraft's **pre-flight checklist for the next flight intent** — the prototype's `0 / 22 items done`. With no flight intent, no active aircraft, or no such checklist, it states that in text rather than showing a favorable or invented count.
- **Localized URL segments.** `nav.segment.checklists` is currently the English word `checklists` in all three catalogs. It becomes `listas` (es), `listas` (pt), `checklists` (en), with new `checklist.segment.*` sub-segments for the run, edit, and history sub-routes, following `fleet.segment.*` and `risk.segment.*`. Not breaking: the old path only ever served the placeholder.
- **i18n**: the prototype's `cl.*` keys (including `cl.items`, a JSON blob of sample item text) are replaced by label-and-message-only keys across `es`/`en`/`pt`; the seeded template's item text moves into a versioned, code-owned seed module carrying its own three-locale content, not into the UI catalogs.
- **Tests**: happy path (seed on aircraft creation, browse, run, toggle, complete, edit, history), sad path (unauthenticated, cross-pilot access, no aircraft, no flight intent, unknown checklist, toggling an item of a completed run, deleting a checklist that has runs), and evals (generic-template caveat present until edited; emergency lists render no checkbox and no progress; a completed run's text is immune to later edits; 44px touch targets; fragment and full page render the same partial; all chrome strings from the catalogs).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `aircraft-checklists`: the per-aircraft checklist library — seeding the generic template on aircraft creation, the normal/emergency grouping, pilot ownership and per-pilot scoping, checklist and item CRUD with ordering, the generic-content caveat, the emergency reference-only contract, and the empty state.
- `checklist-runs`: running a normal checklist — starting a run against a flight intent, the item snapshot, persisting each toggle, the completion counter and completed state, reset, retention of completed runs, the history view, and the progress figure the home tile reads.

### Modified Capabilities
- `feature-scaffolding`: the `checklists` module's `register` callback no longer uses `registerPlaceholderScreen`. The placeholder contract still governs `documents`, the last remaining placeholder; the no-fabrication rule now applies to the checklists screen's own content.
- `home-dashboard`: the Checklists tile stops reporting the capability as unavailable and reports the pre-flight run's completion count for the next flight intent, with an explicit not-available state for every case where there is no such run. **Depends on `inicio-page` landing first** — `home-dashboard` exists today only as a delta spec under `openspec/changes/inicio-page/`.

## Impact

- **Design assurance tier**: **Tier 2 (operational record-keeping)** for the library, runs, and history — happy/sad path tests required, evals included. The **emergency reference content is held to the Tier 1 no-fabrication bar**: seeded emergency procedures are generic and must be labeled as such in text on every screen that displays them, and no screen may present them as the aircraft's certificated procedures. The home tile's figure is Tier 3 chrome sourced from a Tier 2 record.
- **Schema**: two new migrations — `011_checklists.sql` (`checklists` + `checklist_items`, `(id, pilot_id)` composite-unique and composite foreign keys against `aircraft`, following `004`/`009`) and `012_checklist_runs.sql` (`checklist_runs` + `checklist_run_items` carrying the snapshot), with the backfill of existing aircraft in `011`. Ownership columns, UTC timestamps, and the updated-at trigger per the standing convention.
- **Affected code**: `src/modules/checklists/index.ts` (placeholder → real registration), new `src/modules/checklists/{paths,forms}.ts`, new `src/platform/checklists/{checklist-repo,run-repo,template,types}.ts`, new `src/views/pages/checklists*.njk` partials, `src/platform/i18n/chrome.ts` (segments), `src/platform/i18n/catalogs/{es,en,pt}.json`, `src/modules/dashboard/service.ts` (`checklistsTile`), `src/modules/modules.test.ts` (exclude `checklists` from the placeholder-only eval, as `dashboard`, `weather`, `fleet`, and `risk` already are).
- **Reused without modification**: `aircraft-repo` (fleet list, active aircraft), `flight-intent-repo` (next intent), `createRequireAuthHook`, the fragment-rendering convention in `src/server/views/views.ts`, and the existing card/badge/`emptyState` partials.
- **No new dependencies and no configuration change.**
- **Out of scope**: importing checklists from a POH/AFM PDF (belongs to the documents/AIS capability); sharing or publishing checklists between pilots; offline or in-flight availability of emergency procedures (no service worker and no offline story exist yet); checklist content shared per aircraft *type* rather than per aircraft instance; voice or hands-free operation; attaching a completed run to a logbook entry.
