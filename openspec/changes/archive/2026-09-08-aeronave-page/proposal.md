## Why

The `/aeronave` destination (`fleet` module) is still the shared placeholder screen: it renders a localized "not yet available" notice and, by the placeholder contract, may not show a single hour, registration, or due date. A pilot therefore has nowhere in the application to say *which* aircraft they fly, and nothing the pre-flight decision the app exists to support can be anchored to — the shell's "Aeronave Activa" control has no aircraft to name, the risk and checklist modules have no airframe to reason about, and there is no record of flights flown. Every downstream capability needs an aircraft identity and a flight history before it can be more than a weather viewer.

This change makes `/aeronave` the pilot's system of record for the aircraft they operate and the flights they fly in them.

## What Changes

- **Aircraft records.** A pilot can add, edit, and retire any number of aircraft, each identified by registration (ICAO-format tail mark), ICAO type designator, manufacturer, model/variant, and serial number, with class/category (SEP land, MEP land, TMG…), engine and propeller type, and an optional nickname. Registration is unique per pilot.
- **Active aircraft.** Exactly one aircraft may be designated active per pilot; the shell header's "Aeronave Activa" control renders it and offers a switch. The designation is persisted on the pilot and readable by every other module.
- **Airworthiness and documents panel.** Per-aircraft validity records — Certificate of Airworthiness, ARC (Airworthiness Review Certificate) expiry, registration certificate, insurance, radio station licence, noise certificate, mass & balance sheet, ELT registration — each with an issue/expiry date and an expiring-soon/expired indicator derived from the date rather than typed in.
- **Weight & balance profile.** Per-aircraft empty weight, empty-weight arm/moment, MTOW, MLW, maximum zero-fuel weight, usable fuel and its arm, an ordered list of load stations (name, arm, maximum weight), and CG envelope points. Stored and edited here; the actual W&B computation is deferred.
- **Flight logbook.** One entry per leg, owned by the pilot and linked to one of their aircraft, carrying **both** the aircraft-side counters (Hobbs out/in, tach out/in, fuel uplift and burn, day/night landings, passengers) **and** the EASA FCL.050 pilot columns (departure/arrival aerodrome and UTC time, single-engine/multi-engine, function time as PIC/SPIC/dual/co-pilot/instructor, night time, IFR time, remarks, and separately recorded FSTD sessions with device type and qualification number). Entries are created, edited, and deleted from `/aeronave`, with the aircraft's Hobbs/tach pre-filled from the previous entry.
- **Derived totals, never typed.** Airframe total time, engine time, and landings are computed from the logged entries plus a per-aircraft starting offset (the hours the airframe had when the pilot began logging); pilot totals per function, class, night, and IFR, and recent-experience windows (90-day landings, 90-day night landings) are computed from the same entries. No total is ever a stored, hand-edited number.
- **Maintenance schedule.** Per-aircraft maintenance and inspection items — annual/ARC, 50h and 100h services, oil change, ELT battery, transponder and pitot/static certification, life-limited components — each due at a calendar date, at an airframe/engine hour reading, or both; the screen shows time and hours remaining, computed from the logbook, with a due-soon/overdue state conveyed in text as well as color.
- **Engine data import and per-flight trends.** An import seam accepts an avionics engine-monitor data file (CSV export from a JPI EDM / Garmin / Dynon-class monitor) attached to one flight entry, parses its per-sample CHT/EGT/oil-temperature/oil-pressure/RPM/fuel-flow channels, and renders the last flight's engine trend strip. The strip renders **only** when a real file backs it, always labeled with its source file, device, and import time; with no file imported, the panel states that no engine data has been imported — never a placeholder value.
- **Data foundation.** New forward-only migrations for `aircraft`, `aircraft_documents`, `aircraft_load_stations`, `flight_entries`, `maintenance_items`, and `engine_data_files`/`engine_data_samples`, each pilot-owned and ownership-scoped per the identity-access contract.
- The `fleet` module stops calling `registerPlaceholderScreen` and registers real routes, following the existing one-handler/two-presentations htmx fragment convention.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `aircraft-fleet`: the aircraft record itself — creation, editing, retirement, per-pilot registration uniqueness, the active-aircraft designation, the airworthiness/document validity panel, and the weight & balance profile. *(Design assurance Tier 2, with the document-expiry indicators held to Tier 1's no-fabrication rule since they feed a go/no-go judgement.)*
- `flight-logbook`: the flight entry — its aircraft-side and FCL.050 pilot-side fields, validation, edit/delete, per-aircraft and per-pilot derived totals, and recent-experience windows. *(Tier 2.)*
- `maintenance-tracking`: maintenance and inspection items, their date- and hours-based due conditions, and the remaining-time/remaining-hours countdown derived from logged flights. *(Tier 2, with the no-fabrication rule applied to every countdown.)*
- `engine-data-import`: the avionics engine-monitor file import seam — accepted formats, channel mapping, per-flight association, provenance metadata, and the rule that no engine value is displayed without an imported file behind it. *(Tier 1 — operational-looking engine values.)*

### Modified Capabilities
- `feature-scaffolding`: the `fleet` module's `register` callback no longer uses `registerPlaceholderScreen`, so the "placeholder screens state their status honestly" requirement no longer governs `/aeronave`; and the shell header gains a requirement that the active-aircraft control renders the pilot's designated active aircraft (or an explicit "none selected" state) rather than being inert.

## Impact

- **Affected code**: `src/modules/fleet/index.ts` (placeholder → real registration) plus new `src/modules/fleet/` internals (routes, form parsing, service, view models); new fleet data access under `src/platform/fleet/`; `src/platform/i18n/catalogs/{es,en,pt}.json` (a substantial block of new keys); new `src/views/pages/aircraft.njk` and its form/list partials; `src/views/partials/nav.njk` and the layout header for the active-aircraft control; `src/modules/types.ts` if the module context needs a fleet store.
- **Database**: new migrations `004`–`00N` (forward-only, uuid PKs, UTC `created_at`/`updated_at`, the `set_updated_at` trigger, non-nullable `pilot_id` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`), plus seed additions for a development aircraft and a handful of flights, explicitly labeled as sample data.
- **Tests**: happy/sad/eval coverage per the working agreement, including evals that no engine, maintenance, or document value renders without provenance; that ownership scoping returns 404 (never 403) for another pilot's aircraft or flight; that every total is derived rather than stored; that all three catalogs have identical key sets; and that the screen is usable at 320px with 44px touch targets and no JavaScript.
- **Dependencies**: `@fastify/multipart` for the engine-data file upload is the one plausible addition (decided in design); CSV parsing is small enough to implement in-repo rather than pull a parser.
- **Out of scope**: performing the weight & balance calculation or rendering the CG envelope chart (the profile is stored here, computed later); charting engine trends over more than the most recent flight; maintenance work orders, invoices, or CRS/release-to-service signatures; sharing an aircraft between pilots or club/fleet multi-tenancy; importing an existing logbook from another application; exporting an FCL.050-format printable logbook.
